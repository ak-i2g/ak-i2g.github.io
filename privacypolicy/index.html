Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Illusion Interactive ("we", "our", or "us") develops and publishes mobile games and applications. This Privacy Policy applies to our mobile games and applications, including Word Search & Chill, and explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you use them.

Information We Collect

1. Device & Technical Information

  • Device type, operating system, and version
  • Unique device and app identifiers, including advertising identifiers where legally permitted and subject to applicable consent and device settings
  • IP address (used for analytics, fraud prevention, and regional features)

2. Usage & Analytics Data

  • Gameplay events and app interactions
  • Session duration and feature usage
  • In-app behavior and navigation patterns

3. Crash & Performance Data

  • Crash logs and error reports
  • App version, device model, operating system details, and diagnostic information
  • Installation identifiers, performance diagnostics, and stability metrics

Advertising

Our apps may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Depending on your region, consent choices, device settings, and applicable law, these advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized. Personalized ads may be selected using information about your interests or activity. Non-personalized ads are not selected using your past behavior, although Google may still use identifiers or similar technologies for purposes such as frequency capping, aggregated ad reporting, security, and fraud prevention.

AdMob may process advertising identifiers where legally permitted, IP address, IP-derived approximate location, device and app information, and interactions with advertisements to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud.

Learn more about how Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Consent & Privacy Choices

Where required, we use Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present privacy notices, request and record consent choices, and make privacy options available. Your choices may determine whether personalized, non-personalized, limited, or technical ads are served. Where an in-app privacy options entry point is available, you may use it to review or change your choices. You may also manage advertising identifiers, tracking permissions, and other app permissions through your device settings.

Analytics & Crash Reporting

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with our apps. Firebase Analytics may collect gameplay events, sessions, app interactions, engagement information, and device and app information.

We use Firebase Crashlytics to identify and diagnose crashes. Crashlytics may process crash stack traces, installation identifiers, app version, device model, operating system information, timestamps, and other diagnostic data. We use this information to understand usage, fix bugs, improve stability, and optimize app performance.

Learn more about Firebase privacy and security: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

In-App Purchases

Our apps may offer in-app purchases. All purchases are processed securely by the respective platform providers:

  • Google Play Store (Android)
  • Apple App Store (iOS)

We do not collect or store payment details such as credit card numbers. All payment information is handled directly by the platform providers.

How We Use Information

  • Operate and maintain our apps
  • Improve gameplay, features, and user experience
  • Analyze usage, performance, and stability
  • Serve and measure advertisements
  • Ensure security and prevent fraud
  • Comply with legal and platform requirements

Children’s Privacy

Word Search & Chill is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13, or below the applicable minimum age in their country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from such children. If we learn that we have collected such information, we will take reasonable steps to delete it. Parents or guardians may contact us at support@illusioninteractive.games.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information for money. We may disclose information to service providers and partners that help us operate, analyze, secure, and monetize our apps, including:

  • Advertising partners (Google AdMob)
  • Analytics and crash-reporting providers (Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics)
  • Platform providers (Google Play, Apple App Store)

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Depending on where you live, disclosures for personalized or targeted advertising may be treated as a “sale,” “sharing,” or targeted advertising under applicable privacy law, and you may have the right to opt out.

Your Rights & Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information; object to or restrict certain processing; opt out of targeted advertising; or withdraw consent. You may also reset or limit advertising identifiers and manage app permissions through your device settings. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that occurred before the withdrawal.

To exercise a privacy right, email support@illusioninteractive.games or visit our Data Deletion Request page. We may need to verify your request, and some rights may be limited by applicable law.

Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our apps, fulfill the purposes described in this policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and prevent fraud. Retention periods may vary by data type and service provider. When information is no longer required, we delete or anonymize it where reasonably practicable.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
