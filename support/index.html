Support

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Need a hand with Word Search & Chill? Find troubleshooting steps, purchase help, and ways to contact the studio below.

How We Can Help

  • Questions about features or gameplay
  • Technical issues, crashes, or performance problems
  • Billing or purchase questions (handled by platform providers)
  • Player ID, saved game data, and privacy questions

Contact Support

The fastest way to reach us is by email:
support@illusioninteractive.games

For quicker assistance, include:

  • Name of the app and its version
  • Device model and OS version
  • Platform (Android or iOS)
  • A brief description of the issue and steps to reproduce

FAQ

  • I made a purchase but don’t see it. Check that the transaction completed and that you are signed in with the same store account. To restore an eligible No Ads purchase, use the app’s purchase-restoration option. If the issue persists, email support with your app version and purchase receipt.
  • The app is crashing or freezing. Try the troubleshooting steps below. If it continues, email us with your device details and what you were doing when it happened.
  • How do I request data deletion? Follow the instructions on our Data Deletion page. Include the User ID from the app’s Settings page so we can identify your game data.

Troubleshooting Steps

  • Restart the app, then your device if the issue persists
  • Update the app to the latest version available in the store
  • Ensure you have a stable internet connection
  • Free up storage space if your device is low on space
  • Check that battery saver or data saver modes are not restricting the app
