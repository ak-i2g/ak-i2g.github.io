Your next word is on the board.

A target to reach, a timer to beat, and a few helpful boosts along the way.

  1. Check your target.

    Look above the grid to see what the level asks for. You might need words of a certain length, a target count, or a special goal.

  2. Trace the words you find.

    Swipe through the letters to make a word. Completed words leave a coloured path across the grid, so you can see each find.

  3. Keep an eye on the clock.

    Reach the target before time runs out. If you get stuck, extra time, letter help, and hints can help you through a tricky board.
